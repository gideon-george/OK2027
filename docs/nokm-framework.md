# NDC National OK Movement (NOkM) — Executive Framework

Canonical reference for offices, duties, KPIs, and compliance rules. All seed
data in `data/nokm-structure.json` derives from this document. When the two
disagree, this document wins.

Source: NOkM National Leadership circulars and the National Executives
Strategic Meeting of Sunday 7 June 2026.

**Motto:** *Structure. Mobilize. Secure. Grow. Lead. Deliver.*

---

## 1. What NOkM is

The National OK Movement is an **independent support group** mobilising
grassroots support for **Peter Obi (President)** and **Rabiu Kwankwaso (Vice
President)** — the "OK" in the name — in the 2027 Nigerian general election,
aligned with the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

### Core purpose

1. **Mobilization** — register members, mobilise voters, drive turnout at ward
   and polling-unit level.
2. **Coordination** — maintain a structured ladder from National → Zonal →
   State → LGA → Ward → Unit so no community is left out.
3. **Advocacy & sensitization** — communicate the vision, policies, and
   activities of the principals the movement supports.
4. **Bridge / feedback loop** — carry grassroots concerns back up to leadership
   for informed decision-making.

### Operational principles

- **Voluntary membership.** Anyone who believes in the vision may join. No
  coercion. No membership fee to participate.
- **Non-conflict with party structure.** NOkM works *with* party structures
  where they exist and never replaces elected party executives.
- **Discipline & accountability.** Every appointed executive must show visible
  engagement, mobilisation, and financial accountability at their level.
- **Non-violence.** All activity peaceful, lawful, and within INEC regulations.
- **One person, one position.** No double office-holding across levels.

### Relationship with the NDC

NOkM is **not an organ of the NDC**. It is not funded by the party and does not
issue party directives. Its role is to complement the party by doing the
grassroots work of mobilisation, sensitisation, and voter contact. NOkM aligns
with NDC candidates and programmes, but its internal appointments, structure,
and operations are managed by NOkM leadership.

All materials carry "National OK Movement — NDC NOkM" to distinguish them from
official party materials.

## 2. Command ladder

```
National  →  Zonal  →  State  →  LGA  →  Ward  →  Polling Unit
                                                   + Diaspora (by country)
```

Directives flow downward from the National Coordinator. Reports flow upward
weekly from each level.

**Zones (6):** North Central, North East, North West, South East, South South,
South West — plus a Diaspora directorate.

## 3. National offices

Twenty-five offices are defined by the framework circular. A twenty-sixth,
**National Youth Leader**, exists in practice and appears in the official
unveiling; it is included here and flagged as `framework_addendum: true`.

### 1. National Coordinator
*Scope: national · Rank 1*

Overall leader and chief executive of the movement. Provides vision, direction,
leadership, and policy guidance. Presides over national meetings. Supervises all
National, Zonal, State, LGA, Ward and Unit structures. Approves strategic plans
and programmes. Represents the movement nationally and internationally.

**Duties:** coordinate all national activities; approve appointments and
committees; build strategic alliances and partnerships; ensure organisational
unity and discipline.

**KPIs:** membership growth nationwide; number of functional structures
established; programme implementation success rate; stakeholder engagement rate;
organisational stability index.

### 2. National Secretary
*Scope: national · Rank 2*

Administrative head of the movement; custodian of records and correspondence.

**Duties:** prepare agendas and minutes; maintain the membership database;
issue official communications; coordinate reports from all departments.

**KPIs:** timely circulation of reports; accuracy of records; meeting
implementation rate; response time to correspondence.

### 3. National Publicity Secretary
*Scope: national · Rank 3*

Image management and public communication.

**Duties:** handle media relations; prepare press releases; manage publicity
campaigns; promote organisational achievements.

**KPIs:** media mentions; social media engagement; public awareness growth;
positive brand perception.

### 4. National Financial Secretary
*Scope: national · Rank 4*

Financial administration.

**Duties:** maintain financial records; process payments; prepare financial
statements; track donations and contributions.

**KPIs:** financial reporting accuracy; revenue growth; compliance rate; audit
readiness.

### 5. National Treasurer

## 4. Zonal & state offices

### Zonal Coordinator
*Scope: zonal — one per zone (6)*

**Duties:** zonal structure mapping; state leadership recruitment and
recommendation; vacancy publication and mobilisation; verification and screening;
appointment of state coordinators; state and LGA structure development;
oversight, monitoring and reporting; documentation and record-keeping;
maintenance of the aspirants and candidates database; stakeholder engagement;
official communication compliance; enforcement of directives; reporting
protocol; loyalty and institutional commitment; core-values compliance; daily
platform audit; security and risk management; regular meetings and engagement.

**KPIs:** states covered in zone; state coordinators appointed and vetted; LGA
structures completed; reporting compliance; membership growth in zone.

### State Coordinator
*Scope: state — one per state (36 + FCT = 37)*

**Duties:** build and supervise LGA, ward, and polling-unit structures; drive
membership registration statewide; convene state meetings; enforce national
directives; submit weekly reports; maintain state records.

**KPIs:** LGAs with functional structures; wards covered; polling units covered;
new members registered; meeting attendance; reporting compliance.

### LGA Coordinator
*Scope: lga — one per LGA (774)*

**Duties:** build ward and polling-unit structures; drive local registration;
convene LGA meetings; report weekly to the State Coordinator.

**KPIs:** wards covered; polling units covered; new members registered;
reporting compliance.

### Ward Coordinator
*Scope: ward*

**Duties:** appoint and support polling-unit agents; drive ward registration and
PVC collection; report weekly to the LGA Coordinator.

**KPIs:** polling units covered; members registered; PVC completion rate in ward.

### Polling Unit Agent
*Scope: unit*

**Duties:** know every voter in the unit; drive PVC collection and turnout;
report unit-level status upward.

**KPIs:** members registered in unit; PVC completion rate; turnout on election
day.

## 5. General executive KPIs

Every executive is evaluated **quarterly** on ten measures:

| # | Measure |
|---|---|
| 1 | Membership drive growth |
| 2 | Structural expansion drive |
| 3 | Programme delivery |
| 4 | Meeting attendance (minimum 90%) |
| 5 | Reporting compliance |
| 6 | Communication responsiveness |
| 7 | Financial accountability |
| 8 | Conflict resolution effectiveness |
| 9 | Leadership performance |
| 10 | Ethical compliance |

Executives who fail to meet expectations may be replaced. The purpose of
recording KPIs in this platform is to make that decision defensible against a
record rather than an impression.

## 6. Reporting

### Weekly report template

| Field | |
|---|---|
| 1 | Lead Officer name |
| 2 | Position in NOkM |
| 3 | Support Officer name |
| 4 | Position in NOkM |
| 5 | KPIs |
| 6 | Date |

Sessions are scheduled by week and day in the form `WK:1 Day 12`.

Each appointed lead officer handles the training or lecture session for their
scheduled day. The assigned support officer maintains order and decorum and
ensures the session's standing order is kept until the end.

**Special supervisors:** National Coordinator, National Secretary, National
Editor/Auditor, National Director of ICT/New Media, National Publicity
Secretary, National Woman Leader, National Director of Security & Strategy.

### Weekly operating rhythm

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Politics and marketing the movement — strategising and outreach |
| Tuesday | Politics and strategies to win all polling units for Peter Obi & Kwankwaso |
| Wednesday | Update on how far the movement has covered in the state |
| Thursday | Knowing and checking how many have PVC, and planning for the rest |
| Friday | Marketing of members' personal businesses |
| Saturday | Politics and news trending — current affairs analysis |
| Sunday | Checking upon ourselves — community support and solidarity |

## 7. Security & data protection protocols

From the framework circular:

- Mandatory vetting of all executives.
- Confidentiality agreements for key officers.
- Data protection and privacy compliance.
- Crisis management and emergency response plans.
- Incident reporting and escalation procedures.
- Cybersecurity awareness training.
- Background verification for sensitive appointments.
- Event security planning and crowd management.
- Secure handling of membership databases.
- Continuous risk assessment and threat monitoring.

### How this platform implements them

| Protocol | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Secure handling of membership databases | Supabase row-level security; no member data in the repository |
| Data protection compliance | NDPA 2023 consent capture at registration, versioned and timestamped |
| Access control | Phone-OTP auth; officers see only their own scope |
| Protection of officers | Officer phone numbers never published; public contact goes through a form |
| Minimisation | PVC/VIN numbers are never collected — boolean status only |
